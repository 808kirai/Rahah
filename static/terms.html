Your account

  • One account per person. Keep your password to yourself, you're responsible for what's done with your account.

  • Give a real email you can reach, it's how you get back in if you forget your password.

Using Rahah fairly

  • Each account has a daily allowance so Rahah stays free for everyone. Don't try to get round it, for example with extra accounts.

Staying safe

  • Rahah is an AI, not a person. It can't keep secrets for you or help in an emergency.
  • Don't share your home address, phone number, passwords or photos of yourself in chats.
  • If something online, or anything else, is hurting or worrying you, talk to a parent, teacher or school counsellor. In Kenya you can call Childline Kenya on 116 or Kenya Red Cross on 1199 for free, any time, and 999 or 112 in an emergency.

Answers can be wrong

Rahah's answers come from Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. They're often right but sometimes wrong, out of date, or incomplete. Check anything important against your course material, teachers, or a trusted source, especially before exams. Rahah isn't medical, legal, financial, or professional advice.

Your work

What you put into Rahah, and what Rahah makes for you, is yours. You let Rahah store it and send it to Claude only to answer you and keep your work, as the privacy page explains. Only add files you're allowed to share.

An account that breaks these terms can have its allowance lowered or be turned off. If yours is turned off and you think it's a mistake, email us.
